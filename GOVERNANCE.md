# Governance

## Model

Mandata is a **founder-led open project**:

- **Maintainer:** Shedrack Chiedozie Aji ([@ashedrack](https://github.com/ashedrack)) — final
  authority on direction, merges, and releases.
- **Contributors:** anyone whose PRs are merged. Frequent, high-quality contributors
  may be invited as co-maintainers.

## Decision-making

- Day-to-day: maintainer reviews and merges PRs.
- Direction/spec changes: discussed in issues; maintainer decides.
- Disagreements: resolved by discussion first; maintainer's call is final.

## Becoming a co-maintainer

Demonstrated, sustained contribution (merged PRs, reviews, issue triage) over
~3 months → nomination → invitation. Co-maintainers share merge rights but not
release authority.
